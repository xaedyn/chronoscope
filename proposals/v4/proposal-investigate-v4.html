Why we separate hearing from diagnosis.

Chronoscope measures latency. It does not guess at root cause without proof. Most network tools collapse these two — a single number with a one-line guess attached.

That collapse is the most common way users get misled — a tool sees a slow round and announces "your ISP is degraded," when the upstream CDN is throttling cross-region traffic. We'd rather show you the evidence and let the diagnosis follow the data.
