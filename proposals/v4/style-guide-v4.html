03

Motion · five primitives.

Every animated moment in the system reduces to one of these. All honor prefers-reduced-motion.
01

Camera drift + mouse parallax

Slow continuous orbit at ~0.00005 rad/frame. Mouse offset lerped at 4.5% per frame. Active on every 3D scene.

02

Sweep rotation

Single thin beam rotates once per windowSeconds (default 60). The sweep's angular position feeds ring shaders so the orbit lights up under it.

03

Pulse stamp

Per-vertex age attribute in the shader. New pulses pop to 1.8× scale then decay to base over 2 s.

04

Assembly sequence

Loader counts up 000 → 247 (~1.5s), scene assembles: rings draw outward staggered (~1.8s), pulses materialize, sweep fades in last.

05

Strike-through

Used only on error states. A 1 px crimson line animates across the failed station's name over 600 ms. Symbolic — the brand mark gets crossed out.

Fastly
+

Accessibility

prefers-reduced-motion: reduce collapses (1) to no parallax, (2) to a static notch, (3) to discrete stamps without scale-pop, (4) to instant reveal, (5) to a pre-drawn line.

All motion is GPU-cheap · <2 ms per RAF

06

Atmosphere · depth without gradients.

The brief banned CSS gradient backgrounds (banding on dark) — these are the alternatives we built

Vignette

Radial darkening from transparent at 30% to 0.96 bg at the edges. Layered over every WebGL stage to focus attention on the orbital diagram.

Star field

3D points distributed on a sphere shell at radius 60–120. Color-graded warm-white. Real depth parallax with the camera; not a skybox texture.

Noise overlay

Inline SVG fractalNoise at 0.06 alpha, screen-blended. Adds tactile grain to dark surfaces without CSS gradient banding.

Light leak

Subtle radial wash + a 1 px top-edge highlight in station-B. Used on hero stages and atop bordered cards to suggest off-screen illumination.

08

Usage · do & don't.

Patterns that earn their place in Vessel, and the AI-design defaults we refused
Do
  • Use Hanken 200 at hero scale (240–320 px) when the number IS the page's purpose
  • Let severity color override station hue on individual measurements that crossed a threshold
  • Use mono for every numeric value — even single digits, even inline in body copy
  • Show "Carrier opaque" when servers refuse Timing-Allow-Origin. Never invent phase data.
  • Honor prefers-reduced-motion everywhere — every animation has a static fallback
  • Compose verdicts as sentences with a colored keyword (italic station-B for keyword)
  • Use rule lines over background tints for separation. Cards are bg with borders, not lighter fills.
  • Apply the phosphor light-leak hairline as the system signature on bordered surfaces
Don't
  • Use CSS gradients on the dark canvas — they band visibly. Use noise overlay instead.
  • Add a custom cursor. The brief explicitly refused this.
  • Use bloom postprocessing unless you've verified the CDN-served Three.js addon loads reliably. Match the look with shader brightness.
  • Use literal music notation — clefs, staves, noteheads. Even if "the network is an ensemble" stays in copy.
  • Use purple/pink nebula gradients, spaceship icons, or NASA-LCARS pastiche. Those are the rejected directions.
  • Pre-decide a diagnosis. Always show the shape of evidence and let the user follow it.
  • Animate motion that doesn't carry information. Every primitive on the page does diagnostic work.
  • Use filled icons. Hairline strokes only — line-width 1.2–1.5 px.
