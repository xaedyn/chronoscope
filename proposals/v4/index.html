§ 01·What it is

One question, asked of many endpoints at once.

Is your network slow, or is theirs? Chronoscope answers the only question that matters when latency goes wrong, by probing several endpoints in perfectly synchronized rounds and plotting the responses as concentric orbits around your browser.

When pulses arrive on the beat, the field is healthy. When one station drifts while the others hold tempo, the source is upstream of you — not local. The shape of the signature tells the diagnosis.

Operating principle
Evidence before diagnosis.

§ 03 · How it works
01

The browser fires probes at four endpoints.

Every 1 s, Chronoscope dispatches an HTTP request to each station — Google, Edge, AWS, Fastly — in perfectly synchronized rounds. Each request is a small HEAD with no payload.

02

The responses arrive on their orbits.

The browser measures when each response returns and plots it at the angular position of its round on that station's orbital ring. Radial offset from the path encodes drift from baseline median.

03

The shape tells the diagnosis.

Coordinated pulses on a column = healthy field. Ragged columns = drift. One station consistently late while three hold tempo = isolated upstream slowdown. No interpretation we can't show you.
